Why PinD Exists

Google Maps will give you an address. It won't tell you that the woman behind the counter built this from nothing.

In India, a woman owning a space is still a radical act. Property. Walls. A business that is hers.

Most of these spaces exist only through word of mouth, passed between friends, shared in group chats, whispered at dinner tables. They never make it onto any map that matters.

PinD changes that. We map women-owned spaces so they can be found, celebrated, and supported. Not just by people who already know them, but by every woman looking for exactly what they offer.

This is not a directory. Every pin is a story. Every space here was built by someone who held on anyway.

PinD is a Decoding Draupadi initiative, because the DD community has always run on I know a woman. Now there is a map for it.

These spaces exist. They are just not on any map that matters. Until now.
We map feeling, not just location. The safety, warmth, and intention you sense the moment you walk in.

Word of mouth is the oldest technology we have. PinD is its digital form.
We start where we know. We grow only when it means growing well.

Why It Matters

14% of Indian businesses are women-owned.
This map is for the other 86% to find them.

Women-owned businesses in India are underdiscovered by design. They do not have the ad budgets. They rely on referrals. They grow through trust, not traffic.

PinD gives them something simple and powerful: a place to be found by the women who are already looking for them.

Know a space run by a woman? Pin it. That is how this map grows, not through algorithms or ad spend, but through people who care enough to share a name.
