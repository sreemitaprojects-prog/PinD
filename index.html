Why PinD Exists

Google Maps will give you an address. It won't tell you that the woman behind the counter built this from nothing.

PinD is built so we can celebrate taking up space. So that you can find your spot, where you can be unapologetically yourself.

These are your pockets of comfort, of girlhood, of community and belonging. And importantly, it is a movement. A statement. We are here. And we are going nowhere.

In India, a woman owning a space is still a radical act. Property. Walls. A business that is hers. Every pin on this map is a woman who held one anyway.

These spaces exist. They are just not on any map that matters. Until now.
We map feeling, not just location. The safety, warmth, and intention you sense the moment you walk in.
This is not a listing. Every space here is a story. Every pin is a person.
Word of mouth is the oldest technology we have. PinD is its digital form.
We start where we know. We grow only when it means growing well.
