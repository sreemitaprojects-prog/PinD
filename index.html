Our Manifesto

This map is a love letter to the women who built something anyway.

Not the ones who waited for permission. The ones who unlocked a door, hung a sign, and made a place that felt like theirs, and somehow in doing so, made it yours too.

PinD exists because Google Maps will show you an address, but it will not tell you that you can exhale there. It will not tell you that the woman who owns that cafe sat at that counter for years before she put her name on the door.

We are building a different kind of map. One made of stories, not listings. One that grows the same way trust does: slowly, personally, by word of mouth.

Google Maps will show you an address. It will not tell you that you can exhale there.
We map feeling, not just location. Safety, belonging, and the warmth of a space made with intention.
We are not a directory. Every space here is a story, not a listing.
Word of mouth is our oldest technology. PinD is its digital form.
We start where we know. We grow only when it means growing well.

Why It Matters

To own a space in India,
as a woman,
is quietly radical.

Property. Walls. A room of one's own. These have never been simple things for women in India to hold. Every business on this map is a woman who held one anyway. We think that deserves to be seen.
